# Share the website

Open or send this link to your friend:

https://malharsarwate.github.io/Airline-Reservation-Systemm/

It works on another laptop or phone without your computer running. All frontend pages, CSS, JavaScript, and 3D cabin assets are built and published by the GitHub Pages workflow.

1. Open the link and select **Find your next flight**.
2. Choose a route/date/cabin and then **Choose flight**.
3. Select **Enter demo** and **Start demo**. No real account or password is needed.
4. Enter example traveller names and dates of birth.
5. Select seats manually or open **Help me choose**. Explore **3D cabin** and **Passenger view**.
6. Add extras, approve a mock payment, view the booking and download a demo ticket.
7. Use **Reset demo** to clear the browser's sample changes.

The public version is a frontend demonstration. Its sample data and changes are stored in each visitor's own browser; it does not make real airline reservations, charge money, or connect to your local PostgreSQL database.

The complete Spring Boot / PostgreSQL source remains in the repository for running the full-stack project locally.
